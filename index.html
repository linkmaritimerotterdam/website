Who we are

An informal network, well known in the maritime community.

Since 1989 LINK has brought together young professionals from shipping, offshore, shipbuilding, logistics, insurance and everything in between. Our company and personal members meet every month: sailing trips, BBQs, wine, beer and whisky tastings, excursions to maritime companies, fundraising evenings and of course the New Year's drink.
